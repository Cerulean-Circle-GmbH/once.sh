## Dead is not "grep once"

A raw name scan flags 104; 83 are called through dispatchers:

| Dispatcher | Shape |
|---|---|
| `state` | `private.check.$stageTo` |
| `os.check` | `private.<name>.$OSTYPE` → `.darwin`/`.linux` |
| completion engine (`ng/c2:585`) | `$class.$method.completion.$parameter` |
| `ossh config.set/field.get` | `private.ossh.config.$field.{get,set}` |
| `user` | `private.get.$property` |
| `init/once` | `private.state.$state`, `private.docker.scenario.$action.$DOCKER_SCENARIO` |

`private.isSessionName` is the cautionary case: dead-looking per file, but `otmux.fit` called it —
one word-boundary grep settled it (T04).

## Reduction levers

1. **Delete the 21 dead** (880 → 859); one is already gone (`private.isPaneTarget`).
2. **Inline single-call/thin wrappers** (245 candidates, 86 wrappers) — never test seams or
   documented steps.
3. **Resolve the 295 cross-script privates**: promote to public object.verb or move to the owning
   script; `private.this.*` is really a shared toolkit and should be documented as such
   (`private.this.path.canonical` 63 calls, `private.ossh.ssh` 34, `private.config.homes.list` 28,
   `private.as.user` 26).
4. **Merge OS pairs** (15 pairs → 15) where the bodies are short.
5. **Keep completion hooks** (26) — they are dispatch targets.

## Acceptance

- a repeatable sweep (proposed in the burndown doc) instead of a one-off scan;
- the 21 dead deleted with T09's tool;
- the cross-script rule written into `docs/this.md` (what is public, what is the internal toolkit);
- counts before/after in the ticket.
